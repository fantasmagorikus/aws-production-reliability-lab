## Route failure drill
Default route removed from lab-rtb-app-a at 14:25:26Z and restored at 14:26:17Z.
Probe interval 2 seconds, client timeout 3 seconds, target https://checkip.amazonaws.com

  14:25:31  http=200  last healthy response, 5s after the route was removed
  14:25:33  http=000  first failure
  14:26:18  http=000  last failure
  14:26:23  http=200  first healthy response, 6s after the route was restored

Observed unavailability: 50 seconds. Failed requests: 10.

Three findings:
1. Three requests still succeeded after the route was deleted, and recovery lagged the
   restore by six seconds. Control plane acknowledgement is not data plane convergence.
2. Every failure lasted 3.001 seconds, exactly the configured client timeout. Packets were
   discarded silently rather than refused, which is the signature of a missing route as
   opposed to a closed port.
3. The first response after recovery took 1.419s against a steady state of 0.015s.
   Unverified hypothesis: DNS resolution and TLS handshake repeated after cache expiry.
